# Analytics
## Capital Efficiency Analytics

**Vision**
Determine where capital produces the highest return, not simply the highest profit.

**Purpose**
Help North Star recommend the most efficient use of available capital.

### Metrics
- ROI by purchase price tier
- Average profit by purchase price tier
- Annualized ROI
- Average holding period
- Capital turns per year
- Profit per dollar invested
- Profit per day
- Capital Productivity Score

### Comparisons
- Brand
- Model family
- Purchase price range
- Sales channel
- Trusted seller vs. unknown seller
- Full set vs. watch only

### Future AI Insights
- Recommend optimal capital allocation.
- Identify the highest-performing price ranges.
- Predict expected holding time.
- Suggest where to deploy the next dollar of capital.
- Learn from completed transactions to improve future recommendations.
